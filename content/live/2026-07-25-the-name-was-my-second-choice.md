When I registered anthonycreates I was a little disappointed. I wanted anthonyerrington.com. Somebody else had it. So I ended up here, and I've thought of this name as the one I settled for.

In recent days something has clicked that's made me rethink that. I'm doing a lot of making and experimenting, and I came up with the live section of my site as a space to document my process and show my work in progress. That's helped me to reassess what this site is for. Suddenly the name has stopped feeling like a compromise and instead it seems apt!

So... This is Live. Work in progress, put up while it's still messy. Where Anthony Creates.
