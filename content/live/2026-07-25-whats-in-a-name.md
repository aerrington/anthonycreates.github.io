When I registered anthonycreates.com, I was a little disappointed that anthonyerrington.com was already taken. So this was very much a second choice.

However... the name is growing on me. I like creating things. When I came up with the idea for a "Live" corner of my site — a place to document my process, my ideas and my experiments, and to share work in progress as it comes to life — I realised that the choice to register anthonycreates.com feels increasingly apt.

So that's what this space is: Anthony Creates... Live! Work in progress, shared in all its messy process of becoming.
