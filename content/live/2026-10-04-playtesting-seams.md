Seams is my daily puzzle game about drawing the dividing lines in a room of pentominoes, snuggled up together for a cosy night's sleep!

I shared it for the first time with some friends who kindly help out with playtesting on new projects. I got some really positive feedback, as well as ideas for how to make the concept and tutorial a lot clearer. Thanks, guys!

However, later I handed my phone to someone at an improv workshop. Watching them play for the first time was even more useful. There's something about being given a window into someone's process of discovery and figuring things out for the first time. The assumptions they make... or don't.

It became clear to me that things still weren't as clear and obvious as I had assumed. I think the tutorial is finally getting close to where it needs to be, thanks to these different perspectives and sources of feedback.

You can play Seams here: [seams.day](https://seams.day).
